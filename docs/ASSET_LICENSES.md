## Original artwork

The [jo. mark](../assets/brand/jo-mark.svg), theme-aware headers, project cards and five category symbols (`sql`, `code`, `workflow`, `terminal`, `assistant`) are original artwork created for this profile. Illustrations are conceptual, not project screenshots or evidence of performance. The PNG header fallback is a raster rendering of the light header SVG. No downloaded fonts are bundled.

The `assistant` symbol is a generic category icon, not an OpenAI, Anthropic or Google mark. Product names and icons identify their stated usage context; they imply no endorsement, partnership or certification.
